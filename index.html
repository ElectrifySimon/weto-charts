How the numbers were produced

  • The figures in the report are vector drawings, so their text and geometry are both in the PDF. Bar heights, marker positions and printed labels were read from that layer rather than estimated by eye.
  • Where a stacked bar prints only a column total, the segment heights were scaled so the column matches the printed total.
  • Each card states its own method. Nothing here is a modelled estimate of mine.
